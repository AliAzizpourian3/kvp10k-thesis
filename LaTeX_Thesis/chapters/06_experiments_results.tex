\section{Evaluation Scope and Evidence}
\label{sec:story-overview}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

This chapter first reports the reconstructed Mistral-7B reference results, then
summarises the development evidence for the discriminative system, and finally
evaluates the final LayoutLMv3-based span-level discriminative model (V4).
Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology} defines the model components and
Chapter~\ref{ch:implementation} gives the implementation and training setup.
In brief, V1 forms relation candidates between individual \texttt{KEY} and
\texttt{VALUE} tokens. V2, V3, and V4 form candidates between contiguous entity
spans. V3 is a predicted-span diagnostic variant; it has no headline benchmark
result.

The released KVP10k evaluator distinguishes three entry categories. A Regular
entry contains a key and a value, an Unkeyed entry contains only a value, and an
Unvalued entry contains only a key. A prediction can match a target only when
both belong to the same category. Text matching requires normalised edit
distance (NED) below $0.2$. Location matching uses the released-code criterion
of intersection over union (IoU) greater than or equal to $0.3$. Text+location
matching requires both conditions. Matching is greedy and one-to-one.
Precision and recall are calculated per page and macro-averaged over pages with
non-empty ground truth for the selected category. F1 is the harmonic mean of
the two macro averages. Chapter~\ref{ch:implementation},
Section~\ref{sec:evaluation-protocol}, gives the full evaluation procedure.

The direct comparison between the reconstructed Mistral system and V4 uses the
same 581 prepared pages from the official KVP10k test split. This subset is
approximately 55\% of the 1{,}051 official test pages because PDF retrieval and native-text
preparation exclude unavailable files and pages without a usable text layer.
The preparation flow and split isolation are described in
Chapter~\ref{ch:dataset} and Section~\ref{sec:data-preprocessing}. Published
KVP10k results use the coverage reported by Naparstek et al.~\cite{kvp10k} and
are included only as external context.

Three evidence types occur in this chapter. Final test metrics answer the main
system-comparison question. Validation results document model development and
checkpoint selection. Focused diagnostics inspect candidate construction and
relation supervision; their pooled counts are not page-macro benchmark scores.
The comparative geometry analysis uses the frozen, training-fitted clustering
from Section~\ref{sec:layout_clustering}. Those exploratory strata describe annotation geometry and have weak
separation. They do not represent verified document genres.

\section{Discriminative-Model Development}
\label{sec:exp2}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Entity-Classification Initialisation}
\label{sec:res2}

The discriminative development path starts with the linker disabled. The
LayoutLMv3 encoder and thesis-specific classifier learn the token labels
\texttt{KEY}, \texttt{VALUE}, and \texttt{O}, where \texttt{O} marks tokens
outside the two entity classes. The selected entity-pretraining checkpoint is
epoch~\VFourEntityPretrainEpoch{} and has validation \texttt{KEY}/\texttt{VALUE} micro F1
\VFourEntityPretrainMicroFOne{}. Test data was not accessed during this phase.

This result shows that the final input path can learn both entity classes. It
measures token classification on validation data and does not measure complete
key--value extraction. The selected encoder and classifier initialise V4; the
relation linker starts with new weights. Section~\ref{sec:two-phase} describes
this two-phase procedure.

\subsection{Token-Level Candidate Construction (V1)}
\label{sec:exp3}
\label{sec:res3}

V1 forms one relation candidate for each retained \texttt{KEY} token and each
retained \texttt{VALUE} token. If a page contains $N_K$ key tokens and $N_V$
value tokens, the linker receives $N_KN_V$ token-pair candidates, where $N_K$
and $N_V$ are the two retained token counts on that page. This product can be
large while only a small part of the matrix represents annotated relations.
The loss and class-weighting method are defined in
Section~\ref{sec:loss-function}.

No valid page-macro benchmark result is retained for V1. Its role in the thesis
is to identify the dense token-candidate space that motivated span-level
candidate construction.

\subsection{Span-Level Candidate Construction (V2) and Predicted-Span Diagnostic (V3)}
\label{sec:exp4}
\label{sec:res4}

V2 groups contiguous same-label tokens into entity spans before it forms
relation candidates. With $S_K$ retained key spans and $S_V$ retained value
spans, the candidate count becomes $S_KS_V$. Here, $S_K$ and $S_V$ are normally
smaller than $N_K$ and $N_V$. The first V2 path formed training spans from
predicted entity labels while ground-truth annotations supplied the relation
labels. A later teacher-forced V2 configuration formed spans from ground-truth
labels during training and predicted labels during validation. Both V2 paths
initialised the encoder and classifier with weights from the preceding
entity-classification run.

V3 loaded the teacher-forced V2 checkpoint, froze the encoder and classifier,
and trained only the linker with predicted spans. The retained V3 records do
not support a scoped headline benchmark result, so V3 documents only this
diagnostic path. V4 uses ground-truth spans during training and predicted spans
during validation and inference. This difference in span source remains a
limitation of the final method.

\section{Diagnostic Pipeline Analysis}
\label{sec:exp567}
\label{sec:res567}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following analyses use a preserved V2 checkpoint and 200 documents from
the fixed validation split. The relation-label audit uses 20 of those documents.
Each analysis states its unit because logits, word links, token-pair cells, and
span-pair labels are different quantities.

\subsection{Relation-Logit and Probability Distribution}

For a candidate pair $(i,j)$, the linker produces an unbounded relation logit
$\ell_{ij}$. The sigmoid function converts it to a probability,
$p_{ij}=\sigma(\ell_{ij})$. The decoder can retain a relation only when its
probability reaches the fixed threshold of $0.5$. The relation-logit function is
defined in Section~\ref{sec:kv-attention}.

Across 1{,}481 historical V2 candidate-pair logits, the median is $-6.93$ and
the mean is $-7.59$. A median logit of $-6.93$ corresponds to a sigmoid
probability of approximately $0.001$. Only 36 candidates, or 2.4\%, reach
$p_{ij}\geq0.5$, and only 20 best-per-key predictions reach that threshold.
Thus, the checkpoint suppresses most candidate links and produces sparse
decoded output. This distribution describes the symptom; it does not identify
its cause or measure the gain from a later correction.

\subsection{Bounding-Box Filtering}

The historical V2 span grouper retained a token only when its box met
$(x_1<x_2)\wedge(y_1<y_2)$. In that path, the prepared $0$--$100$ coordinates
were incorrectly rescaled by the page dimensions before they entered the
model. This extra quantisation could produce zero-width or zero-height
model-input boxes, which the strict predicate then removed from the candidate
entities. For
class $c\in\{\texttt{KEY},\texttt{VALUE}\}$, the diagnostic removal rate is

\begin{equation}
  r_c = 1 - \frac{N_c^{\mathrm{strict}}}{N_c^{\mathrm{before}}},
\end{equation}

where $N_c^{\mathrm{before}}$ is the number of predicted class-$c$ tokens before
the predicate and $N_c^{\mathrm{strict}}$ is the retained count. For
\texttt{KEY}, the counts are 3{,}490 and 1{,}832, so $r_{\texttt{KEY}}=47.5\%$.
For \texttt{VALUE}, the counts are 12{,}649 and 6{,}514, so
$r_{\texttt{VALUE}}=48.5\%$. Only 81 of 200 pages retain at least one strict key
span and one strict value span.

The predicate therefore removes about half of both predicted entity-token
types and creates a concrete candidate-construction failure. The final span
grouper excludes only all-zero special or padding positions. The separate final
input-coordinate correction is documented in
Section~\ref{sec:image-processing}. These changes enter the final pipeline
together, so this diagnostic does not measure the independent performance gain
from the relaxed predicate.

\subsection{Relation-Label Construction}

For a recovered Regular relation $r$, let $K_r$ be its aligned key-word indices
and $V_r$ its aligned value-word indices. The historical construction created
the Cartesian product

\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}^{\mathrm{old}}_r = K_r \times V_r,
\end{equation}

which marks every key-word and value-word combination as positive. The final
construction selects one representative aligned word pair for each recovered
relation. Subword tokenisation can expand one word pair into several positive
token-pair cells, and span collapse then maps those cells to span-pair labels.

Table~\ref{tab:diagnostic-verification} traces these units for the 20-document
audit. The sequence is 79 ground-truth Regular pairs, 53 recovered
representative word links, 453 positive token-pair cells after subword
expansion, and 44 positive span-pair labels after span collapse.

\begin{table}[ht]
  \centering
  \caption{Relation-label units in the 20-document validation audit.}
  \label{tab:diagnostic-verification}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{5.8cm}p{3.7cm}r@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Quantity} & \textbf{Unit} & \textbf{Count} \\
    \midrule
    Ground-truth Regular entries & Key--value pairs & 79 \\
    Recovered representative relations & Word-level links & 53 \\
    Positive labels after subword expansion & Token-pair cells & 453 \\
    Positive labels after span collapse & Span-pair labels & 44 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The counts show why word links, token-pair cells, and span-pair labels cannot be
used as interchangeable units. They verify the units produced by the corrected label construction in this
20-document audit.
The final V4 lineage combines this construction with the span-filter and
coordinate corrections, so the evidence cannot assign an independent
performance effect to one correction.

\section{Final V4 Evaluation}
\label{sec:exp8}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

This section is the main evaluation of the final discriminative system. The
validation Regular text+location macro F1 selected checkpoint~15 before test
evaluation. The selected checkpoint was then evaluated once on the 581 prepared
test pages. Test metrics were not used for checkpoint selection or early
stopping. Table~\ref{tab:v4-results} gives validation and test F1,
Table~\ref{tab:main-system-comparison} gives the same-page Mistral comparison,
and the later tables separate entity classification, output coverage, geometry
strata, and qualitative observations.

\subsection{Validation Selection and Test Result}

Checkpoint~\VFourBestEpoch{} has validation Regular text+location F1
\VFourValCombinedFOne{}. Table~\ref{tab:v4-results} reports its Regular
validation and test results.

checkpoint~15. The transferred entity-pretraining checkpoint was selected
separately at its epoch~10.
